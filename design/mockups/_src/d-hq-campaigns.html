HQ · dashboard & campaign builder

HQ steers what stores focus on. The builder protects store attention and sets up honest measurement.

  1. Store workload is a headline numberMissions per store per day sits next to impact and acceptance, so HQ sees the cost of adding more work alongside the benefit.Fixes: alert fatigue starting at HQ (P1)
  2. Four short stepsGoal, stores, message, review. Drafts save automatically, and the current step is shown with a number and a label.Fixes: campaigns that are hard to set up
  3. Pick stores with readable rulesEach rule reads like a sentence and can be removed, with the matching store count updating live. Exclusions such as refits are rules too, not a manual list.Fixes: campaigns sent to stores they don't fit (P1, P6)
  4. Mission budget checkBefore launch, HQ sees how many stores hit their daily limit and what would be pushed back, and can start them later instead.Fixes: alert fatigue (P1)
  5. Comparison group set at launchComparison stores are picked at random up front, so the reported lift means something.Fixes: trust in impact numbers (P5)
  6. Live store previewHQ sees the exact card a manager will get, including the per-store "why", before anything is sent.Fixes: unclear why, the gap between HQ and stores (P2, P6)
  7. Store feedback comes back to HQRejection reasons are counted and can be passed to the right team. Stores can see that their feedback lands somewhere.Fixes: the gap between HQ and stores (P6)
